              <h1>
                                  Russian Missile Identified in Kyiv Children’s Hospital Attack
                              </h1>
            </div>
            
            
                          <div class="singular__content__text__meta">
                <div class="meta meta">

    <time datetime="July 9, 2024" class="meta__time">
    July 9, 2024
  </time>
  
  <div class="meta__tags">
        <ul>
            <li><a href="/tag/missile">Missile</a></li>
            <li><a href="/tag/russia">Russia</a></li>
            <li><a href="/tag/ukraine">Ukraine</a></li>
          </ul>
  </div>
</div>
              </div>
            
            <div class="singular__content__text__content">
              
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An analysis of open source evidence, as well as missile experts, have pointed to a Russian launched Kh-101 cruise missile being the weapon that struck a children’s hospital in Kyiv, debunking claims from pro-Russian accounts and actors that denied responsibility and sought to shift the blame for the incident on to Ukraine.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The July 8 attack on the Okhmatdyt Children’s Hospital <a href="https://www.facebook.com/share/p/7qtQnWmyiTiwJ19F/">killed two</a> and injured over 50 others, according to the hospital. However, the full toll of dead and injured remains unknown with individuals <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/article/2024/jul/08/rescuers-search-rubble-of-kyiv-childrens-hospital-after-russian-missile-attack-in-ukraine">reported</a> to still be trapped under rubble.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the aftermath of the strike, several social media accounts known for spreading disinformation — including that of the <a href="https://x.com/mfa_russia/status/1810307880244965438">Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs</a> — began to claim that the missile was American-made and that it had been launched from a Ukrainian anti-aircraft missile system.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Yet an analysis conducted by Bellingcat using social media footage as well as&nbsp; a 3D model of the missile, all point to the munition being a <a href="https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/kh-101-kh-102/">Russian Kh-101 cruise missile</a>. The analysis is in line with the view of experts, including <a href="https://x.com/FRHoffmann1/status/1810350033616732653">Fabian Hoffman</a>, a doctoral research fellow at the University of Oslo who specialises in missile technology.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>


<div class="wp-block-image">
<figure class="aligncenter size-full is-resized"><a href="https://x.com/FRHoffmann1/status/1810350033616732653" target="_blank" rel="https://x.com/FRHoffmann1/status/1810350033616732653 noreferrer noopener"><div class="media"><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" src="https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-09-at-05.45.11.png" alt="" class="wp-image-44889" width="295" height="489" srcset="https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-09-at-05.45.11.png 596w, https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-09-at-05.45.11-181x300.png 181w" sizes="(max-width: 295px) 100vw, 295px" /></div></a><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">A screengrab of a tweet by missile technology expert Fabian Hoffman.</figcaption></figure>
</div>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dr. Jeffrey Lewis, a nuclear weapons and missile expert at the Middlebury Institute of International Studies at Monterey in California, similarly concluded in an email to Bellingcat that a Kh-101 missile could be seen in footage of the attack posted to social media sites. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bellingcat also tested an alternative theory floated online that the missile was a US-made AIM-120, but found no evidence for this.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The hospital attack came as part of a wider Russian missile barrage that hit civilian targets elsewhere in Ukraine, including the city of Dnipro. According to <a href="https://t.me/kpszsu/16341">the Ukrainian Air Force</a>, the attack began at approximately 10:00 AM local time and involved at least 36 missiles, including 13 Kh-101 cruise missiles.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Analysing the Missile Strike Footage</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Shortly before noon, <a href="https://t.me/Tsaplienko/56544">reports began to surface</a> claiming that there had been an explosion at the Okhmatdyt Children’s Hospital. These reports were followed by video footage posted to Telegram showing a missile impacting the hospital.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Debunking US Missile Claims</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the aftermath of the explosion, some social media personalities were quick to claim that the missile was not of Russian origin.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Jackson Hinkle, a political activist who has appeared on Russian state media, was one of the individuals who <a href="https://x.com/jacksonhinklle/status/1810294806066626762">made the incorrect claim</a> that the missile was US-made.</p>


<div class="wp-block-image">
<figure class="aligncenter size-full"><div class="media"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="818" height="1122" src="https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-09-at-05.21.31.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-44923" srcset="https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-09-at-05.21.31.jpeg 818w, https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-09-at-05.21.31-219x300.jpeg 219w, https://www.bellingcat.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-09-at-05.21.31-768x1053.jpeg 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 818px) 100vw, 818px" /></div><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Screenshot of a tweet by Jackson Hinkle claiming without evidence that the missile that hit the hospital was “an American Patriot air defense missile”. The Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs </em><a href="https://x.com/mfa_russia/status/1810307880244965438"><em>made a similar claim</em></a><em> later in the day (Source: </em><a href="https://x.com/jacksonhinklle/status/1810294806066626762"><em>X</em></a><em>/Twitter)</em></figcaption></figure>
</div>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Hinkle has been <a href="https://www.conspiracywatch.info/en/who-is-jackson-hinkle-twitters-most-viral-misinformation-spreader-and-anti-israel-activist/">banned</a> from several social media platforms for spreading false information.<br></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Similarly, <em>War on Fakes </em>— widely <a href="https://www.poynter.org/fact-checking/2022/how-war-on-fakes-uses-fact-checking-to-spread-pro-russia-propaganda/">regarded</a> as <a href="https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/files/files/Publications/20230719_NP_VIGINUM_RAPPORT-CAMPAGNE-RRN_EN.pdf">a </a><a href="https://www.logically.ai/resources/russian-war-on-fakes-fact-checking-account">Russian propaganda and disinformation outlet</a> —<em> </em><a href="https://t.me/warfakes/23213">claimed</a> in a visual analysis that given the length-to-width ratio of the missile, it could not be a Kh-101, but instead an <a href="https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104576/aim-120-amraam/">AIM-120</a> fired from a Ukrainian <a href="https://www.rtx.com/raytheon/what-we-do/integrated-air-and-missile-defense/nasams">NASAMS battery</a>. The AIM-120 is produced by the U.S. defence contractor Raytheon Company.<br><br>The claim that the missile was an AIM-120 ignores the jet engine prominently hanging from the empennage of the missile that hit the hospital. This highly visible feature is present on the Kh-101, but not on the AIM-120. Furthermore, the wings in the mid-section of the missile that hit the hospital are a mismatch for those on an AIM-120.<br><br>At the time of writing this article, <em>War on Fakes’</em> Telegram post had nearly 800k views, and thousands of shares.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In a <a href="https://t.me/warfakes/23217">follow-up post</a>, <em>War on Fakes </em>claimed that higher quality stills had been edited to make the jet engine of the Kh-101 more prominent, and that in fact the video had shown an AIM-120. However, the jet engine was still visible in lower quality versions of the video, including the ones used in the analysis in this article.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Another Telegram account, ves.rf (ВЕСЪ.РФ), which has more than 56,000 followers, also made a post attempting to exonerate Russia for the attack. The post, which had been viewed more than 440,000 times and which had been widely reposted on X/Twitter, also claimed that the missile that struck the hospital was an AIM-120. Simultaneously, ves.rf also provided cover for the strike by stating that the children’s hospital was directly next to the Ukrainian State Aviation Service. This would seem to undermine its earlier claims it was not a Russian missile that hit the hospital.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Nevertheless, Bellingcat created a second 3D model to represent an AIM-120 missile to test this theory. When comparing this model to images of the missile that hit the hospital, factors like the forward fins of the AIM-120 not matching were apparent. Additionally, the nose of the AIM-120 is much sharper than that of the missile in the hospital strike video. Perhaps most noticeable is the absence of any component on the AIM-120 matching the jet engine seen at the rear of the missile in the video.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The difference between the features of the AIM-120 and the missile that hit the hospital are also noticeable in this video overlay.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The hospital was far from the only target during the Russian attack of July 8. At least twenty people were killed in strikes across Kyiv alone, with city mayor Vitali Klitschko <a href="https://t.me/vitaliy_klitschko/3268">saying</a> that almost 100 had been injured.<br><br>In total, <a href="https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/ukrainian-air-defence-engaged-repelling-russian-missile-attack-kyiv-officials-2024-07-08/">at least 41 people were killed</a> across the country as Russian missiles fell on several cities aside from Kyiv, including Kryvyi Rih and Dnipro.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This latest wave of Russian attacks against Ukraine is among the largest and deadliest in months. Most recently, the Russian armed forces launched hundreds of missiles and drones at cities across Ukraine on <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/2023/dec/29/russia-launches-huge-wave-missile-strikes-ukraine">December 29 2023</a> and then on <a href="https://kyivindependent.com/update-5-killed-119-injured-in-russian-jan-2-large-scale-attack-against-ukraine/">January 2 2024</a>, killing dozens of people and injuring hundreds.</p>
